About Cool Furman Games

Cool Furman Games is a small browser-game site built around quick, approachable games that are easy to play between classes or whenever you have a few minutes to spare.

What we are building

Our initial goal is to launch with two or three simple games on the front page. The first collection may include arcade-style games such as Snake, Flappy Bird, Tetris, or a small platformer. We are keeping the scope focused so each game feels playable and polished.

During a session, players will be able to create a username and submit high scores. Leaderboards are planned to show submitted scores and the names attached to them. We also plan to support local score storage where the browser allows it.

Planned features

  • Interactive games that users can launch from the main page
  • Question-mark buttons that explain how each game is played
  • Local and website-wide leaderboards for users' high scores
  • A popup that asks users to create a session name for leaderboards
  • A theme dropdown with light mode, dark mode, preset colors, and custom colors
  • A contact option for sharing ideas, complaints, or thanks

These features are part of our planned scope and may change as the site develops.
